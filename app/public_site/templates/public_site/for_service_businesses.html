Keep team responsibilities connected to the job

Assign the work. Document the result.

Managers can add team members and assign jobs. Employees have a focused role: they can view the jobs assigned to them, complete those jobs, and upload before-and-after images as part of the work record.

This keeps the employee workflow narrower than the full Manager workspace while still connecting field work back to the business process.

Know what currently free means

Start without a current platform subscription.

Business registration and platform access are currently free. GetContractorz plans to introduce a subscription model in the future, but no public plan structure, pricing, billing cycle, or transition terms are currently approved.

That means the website should say currently free, not free forever.

Does Marketplace visibility guarantee leads?

No. A registered business may be publicly discoverable, but GetContractorz does not guarantee enquiries, leads, jobs, earnings, or growth.

Is every business manually approved?

No. There is currently no manual business-approval process. A user completes business registration and the account role changes to Manager.

Does GetContractorz verify my license or credentials?

No. GetContractorz does not currently independently verify business identity, licenses, credentials, insurance, or other business claims.

Will I be charged later?

A subscription model may be introduced in the future, but no plan, price, billing arrangement, or transition promise is currently published.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for service-business owners and managers.

How do I become a Manager?

Create an account, complete the business-registration form in the Business tab, and once the business is created your role changes from User to Manager.

Can I add more than one service category?

Managers select the applicable supported service category or categories during business setup and configure the services their business provides.

Can I create different questionnaires for different services?

Yes. Managers can create service-specific questionnaires.

Can I add my own team members?

Yes. Managers can add and manage team members and assign jobs to employees.

Can employees see the entire business workspace?

The verified employee role is narrower than the Manager role. Employees can view and complete assigned jobs and upload before-and-after images.

Can clients see quotes and invoices?

Clients can access relevant quotes and invoices depending on their assigned access and service status.
