About GetContractorz

Built around the real work behind service businesses.

The job itself is only one part of running a service business. Clients, requirements, team responsibilities, job documentation, quotes, invoices, payouts, and business information all need to stay organized around it.

GetContractorz is built to bring those supported workflows into one connected business workspace.

Why the platform exists

Service operations can become fragmented fast.

A client may begin in one system, service requirements in another, job photos in a message thread, and quotes or invoices somewhere else. The more disconnected the workflow becomes, the harder it is to understand what belongs to which client or job.

GetContractorz focuses on operational organization: bringing the supported client, service, team, job, documentation, quote, invoice, payout, and business-information workflows together.

What GetContractorz is building around

A platform for service-business operations - with Marketplace discovery as a separate layer.

The business workspace is the core product. Registered service businesses use it to manage their operations.

The Marketplace is a complementary public layer that lets visitors discover registered businesses and access public contact information. The visitor chooses who to contact, and the service relationship remains between the visitor and the selected business.

Product principles

Built for clarity, structure, and accurate expectations.

Clarity before hype

Explain what the product actually does. Do not replace missing proof with exaggerated claims.

Structured workflows

Keep client, service, team, job, quote, invoice, and related information connected where the product supports it.

Role-appropriate access

Managers, Employees, and Clients have different responsibilities and should not be described as if they use the same workspace.

Direct Marketplace relationships

Marketplace discovery supports direct contact without presenting GetContractorz as the provider, employer, verifier, or contracting party.

Accuracy as a trust signal

No fake testimonials. No invented statistics. No verification badges without verification. No guaranteed outcomes.

Current market

Currently serving Canada and the United States.

Public geographic copy should remain at the country level unless real registered-business data supports a more specific state, province, metro, or city claim.
