What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and tokens used to remember a session, maintain security, or preserve a user choice.

Current functional use

GetContractorz may use browser storage and session technologies that are necessary for authentication, security, form protection, account continuity, and expected interface behaviour. These technologies are used to make requested website and platform functions work.

The public Marketplace may temporarily use session storage to restore a visitor's scroll position after a filter action. This information remains in the browser session and is not a Marketplace profile field.

Third-party technologies

Third-party providers may set or read technologies when their verified services are used, such as payment processing or embedded provider workflows. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

GetContractorz does not state that Google Analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural advertising tools, or a specific consent platform is used unless that implementation is confirmed.

Browser controls and updates

Most browsers allow users to block or remove stored data. Disabling technologies required for authentication or security may prevent parts of the platform from working.

This page will be updated after the production cookie and tracking inventory is approved. Questions may be submitted through the contact page.
