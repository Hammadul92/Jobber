Our approach

GetContractorz aims to make its public content and core workflows understandable, keyboard accessible, responsive, and compatible with common assistive technologies.

Current design practices

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and link text.
  • Visible keyboard focus and controls that do not rely on colour alone.
  • Responsive layouts designed to avoid horizontal overflow.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and hidden treatment for decorative assets.
  • Accessible accordion, navigation, form, and error-message patterns.

Testing and known limitations

The website and platform continue to evolve. Some workflows, third-party services, uploaded content, or legacy interfaces may not yet provide the same level of accessibility. A complete audited conformance claim has not been approved.

Report an accessibility barrier

Use the contact form and describe the page, task, device, browser, and assistive technology involved. Do not include passwords or sensitive account information. GetContractorz will use the information to investigate the issue, but no response-time commitment is currently published.
