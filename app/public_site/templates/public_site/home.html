Manage your business. Be discoverable in the Marketplace.

GetContractorz's core product is the business workspace. The Marketplace is a separate public discovery layer where visitors can browse registered businesses, review public business information, and contact the business they choose directly.

Being listed does not mean GetContractorz has verified, approved, or endorsed the business, and Marketplace visibility does not guarantee leads or service outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is GetContractorz free for service businesses?

Business registration and platform access are currently free. A subscription model may be introduced later, but no future plan, price, or billing promise is currently published.

What kinds of service businesses can use GetContractorz?

GetContractorz currently supports the service categories listed on the Service Categories page, including construction, cleaning, landscaping, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, moving services, pest control, and others.

Does GetContractorz verify businesses in the Marketplace?

No. GetContractorz does not currently independently verify business identity, licensing, credentials, insurance, employee backgrounds, service quality, or other business claims.

Does GetContractorz guarantee Marketplace leads?

No. The Marketplace provides public discovery and direct contact, but it does not guarantee enquiries, leads, responses, jobs, earnings, or business growth.

Can clients use GetContractorz too?

Yes. Clients invited by a registered business can access the parts of the platform relevant to their services, including questionnaires and applicable quotes and invoices.

What can employees do?

Employees can view and complete assigned jobs and upload before-and-after images as part of the job workflow.
